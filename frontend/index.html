WALL-E doesn't know where EVE is.
It knows how much it doesn't know.

Every reading is noisy, every move burns battery, a scan costs three — and EVE keeps moving. The agent carries a probability map over her cell, propagates it through a motion model, corrects it with each observation, and spends energy only where that map says the uncertainty is worth resolving. Then it writes the arithmetic down.

The update

One update, four lines of arithmetic.

There is no learned model here and no black box. Each step pushes the map through a motion model, multiplies it by a likelihood, discounts the cell the agent is standing on, and renormalises — so any single decision can be reconstructed by hand. Scroll: the map on the right is the agent's real posterior at five moments of the recorded run.

  1. 1 · Prior

    Uniform over free cells, then narrowed by the opening bearing (±25°) and strength. The first guess is loose on purpose — 2% at its best cell.

    p₀ ∝ exp(−½·((s − e^(−d/5))/0.08)²)

  2. 2 · Predict

    EVE is alive. The agent believes she takes one step to a free neighbour with probability 0.10 (she actually does it 0.12 of the time), so the belief is smeared across her possible neighbours before every reading.

    p ← (1−q)·p + q·Σ p[x±1,y±1] / deg(x,y)

  3. 3 · Likelihood

    The sensor reports the strongest source, and two known decoy beacons emit too. Where a beacon already explains the reading, the likelihood is flat — the honest way to say "that number was not about her".

    L ∝ exp(−½·((s − max(e^(−d/5), decoy))/σ)²) · σ = 0.17 / 0.09

  4. 4 · Multiply, discount, renormalise

    Obstacles are zero. The cell WALL-E is standing on is discounted, not zeroed — a target that can move is allowed to come back. Clues multiply in a Gaussian of ±3 cells.

    p ← p · L · p[agent] ×= 0.06 · p ← p / Σp

  5. 5 · Spend

    A* walks to the best-smoothed hypothesis. If charge can't cover the trip back to a charged pad plus two scans, it turns back first; if two hypotheses share the mass, it scans to buy a bearing.

    reserve = path(station) + 2 × cost(scan) · cost = 1 / 3

The economy

Information is the scarce resource.

Battery is the only currency and it never comes back by itself. A blocked move costs the same as a good one, which is why the planner carries a reserve instead of an optimism.

move−1×25any of four neighbours; bumping a wall still bills you
scan−3×13the only action that returns a bearing — the ambiguity killer, when a decoy isn't louder
recharge+20×0three charged pads; the route back must stay affordable at all times
clue pickup0×0free, and worth a Gaussian of ±3 cells around EVE
